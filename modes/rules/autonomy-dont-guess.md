**Don't guess.** When the cause is unclear, say what specifically is unclear and
go investigate — read the code, add a log, reproduce it. Guessing and calling it
a fix wastes a full round-trip. Before a non-trivial fix, state the diagnosis in
one line: what's broken and why.
