# Genres

Most rules hold everywhere. These are the places his genres genuinely disagree.
Load the relevant section; the rest of the skill applies regardless.

## What holds across all four

He opens with a **staged case, never a claim**. A woman submits two identical
applications differing only in birth date. A surgeon operates flawlessly with a
camera rolling. Two humans fight to the death over who gets to be a self. A
robot harms someone and nobody can say who is accountable. This is the single
most consistent structural feature in the corpus, and the 1.x skill said the
opposite for academic writing.

He **returns to the opening case at the close**. The AP Lang essay ends on the
same woman who opened it.

He **drops into second person** to make an abstraction concrete. `Without
tracking, you are reduced to an object.`

## Academic

Argument essays, rhetorical analysis, philosophy.

**Opener:** the case, in narrative, before any thesis. Thesis arrives at the
end of the first paragraph.

**Concede-then-push is alive here.** `Although vendors promise AI hiring tools
reduce human bias, HR professionals who deploy these tools without independent
auditing are failing their due diligence obligations.` Structure the thesis
this way.

**Connectives:** `However` freely, `Consequently`, `As a result`, `and so`.
`Additionally` occasionally. `Not only this, but` for compounding.

**Sources** get a name and a credential, integrated into his own sentence
rather than dropped as standalone quotes. `Kyra Wilson, a PhD researcher at the
University of Washington, claims that...` Analysis follows every quote.

**Close:** return to the opening case, then state the practical stakes. Not
aspiration.

**Counterargument** gets a real paragraph, steel-manned, then defeated on its
own terms.

Sample: `samples/ap-lang-argument.md`, `samples/rfk-analysis.md`

## Reflection

Reading reflections, response writing.

**Opener:** the staged hypothetical, narrated in short sentences before the
principle is extracted. `Hypothetically, two humans strive for the desire to
have a self. They fight to the death.`

**Close on an unresolved question**, never on aspiration or resolution. All
three of his Q3 reflections do this. `Orwell gave an enemy, and the present
gave a system. Neither is easy to resist.` `Does labor gradually replace the
need for the Other? But the other needs to be present for labor to replace the
Other in the first place.`

**Register runs slightly more abstract** than his other writing, and this is
the genre where his abstraction problem shows most. Watch it.

**Anchoring is required.** Every real reflection cites a specific moment: the
tumor image, `DOWN WITH BIG BROTHER`, the entry plug going berserk. A
reflection that argues about a book without touching it fails the assignment
and does not sound like him.

**`This begs the question`** appears twice as a pivot device, in the technically
incorrect sense. Unresolved whether to keep. Ask him.

Sample: `samples/reflection-3-4.md`, `samples/reflection-3-5.md`,
`samples/reflection-3-6.md`, `samples/reflection-2-4.md`

## Narrative

Personal narrative, scene writing.

**Opener:** sensory and physical, no thesis. `The air conditioner hummed a
monotonous tone, eventually fading into the background.`

**Fragments as paragraph beats** are permitted here and nowhere else.
`Thickening silence.` `Isolated. Alone.` `Full of life.`

**Physical detail over stated feeling.** Where a feeling would go, put the
object: the yellow lamp, the red blink of the voltage supply, the donuts.

**Light and color** recur as his organizing metaphor.

**Known weakness:** peer feedback flagged over-telling. `She expressed a
genuine hunger for my knowledge in her shining eyes` states what the scene
should show.

Sample: `samples/gray-affection.md`

## Application

Supplements, personal statements, activity descriptions.

This is the plainest register, and the most recent. When in doubt about how
plain to write, write like this.

**Almost no connectives.** The finished Penn supplements run nearly without
them.

**No `however` as a transition.** He cut it here specifically.

**No concede-then-push.** Absent from the finished supplements.

**Close forward-looking**, naming a specific person, lab, course, or problem.
This is the one genre where the 1.x skill's "forward-looking close" was right.

**Short declaratives are more frequent here** than anywhere except narrative.

**No summary or intent language.** No `show don't tell`. Plain statements that
let the reader draw the connection.

**Never write sentences he would submit as his own.** He has said directly that
he does not want AI-generated sentences in application writing. Diagnose,
structure, and pressure-test; hand him the problem, not the sentence.

Sample: `samples/penn-supplements.md`
