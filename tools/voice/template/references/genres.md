# Genres

Empty until `/voice-build {{NAME}}` fills it.

What belongs here: rules that hold in one kind of writing only. One section
per genre that `samples/` actually contains, named by `/voice-build` from the
samples and the interview. A genre with no sample gets no section: a rule
invented for it would be a guess.

For each genre: the register (how formal, which person), what changes from the
rules in `mechanics.md` and `structure.md`, and which sample shows it best.
